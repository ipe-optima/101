### F23-3(b) | Final 2023 | 10
The following sentences are erroneous. Fix the problems and rewrite them. (a) Too much gaming makes children wasting their valuable learning hours. (b) Can we have our building paint by the weekend? (c) His knowledge of languages and international relations aid him in his work. (d) The photograph, an old family secret, are an especially important part of our life. (e) Although there are only two hundred foreign students studying at State University in 2002, there are more than five hundred now. (f) In 1950 it was naively predicted that eight or ten computer would be sufficient to handle all of the scientific and business needs. (g) She crossed the road and hurries away to the crowd. (h) Professor Adrian was so rigorous in editing that until the last moment of publication, he always makes his subordinates reread proof. (i) It is essential that all concerned ready to learn from mistakes. (j) Medical scientists usually recommend that every individual takes vaccination seriously.

#### Answer
- **(a)** Too much gaming makes children **waste** their valuable learning hours.
- **(b)** Can we have our building **painted** by the weekend?
- **(c)** His knowledge of languages and international relations **aids** him in his work.
- **(d)** The photograph, an old family secret, **is** an especially important part of our life.
- **(e)** Although there **were** only two hundred foreign students studying at State University in 2002, there are more than five hundred now.
- **(f)** In 1950 it was naively predicted that eight or ten **computers** would be sufficient to handle all of the scientific and business needs.
- **(g)** She crossed the road and **hurried** away to the crowd.
- **(h)** Professor Adrian was so rigorous in editing that until the last moment of publication, he always **made** his subordinates reread proof.
- **(i)** It is essential that all concerned **be** ready to learn from mistakes.
- **(j)** Medical scientists usually recommend that every individual **take** vaccination seriously.

#### Explanation
- **(a):** *make* + object + base verb (causative), so no *-ing*.
- **(b):** *have* + object + past participle means arrange for something to be done by someone else.
- **(c):** the subject is the singular noun *knowledge*; the phrase "of languages and international relations" does not change the number.
- **(d):** the subject is the singular *photograph*; the phrase between commas is an appositive.
- **(e):** 2002 is in the past, so the verb must be past tense, contrasted with "now".
- **(f):** a plural number (eight or ten) needs a plural noun.
- **(g):** keep the tense consistent with the past tense *crossed*.
- **(h):** the main clause is in the past (*was*), so the verb must also be past (sequence of tenses).
- **(i):** the subjunctive after "It is essential that" uses the base form *be*.
- **(j):** the subjunctive after *recommend that* uses the base form *take*, even with a singular subject.
